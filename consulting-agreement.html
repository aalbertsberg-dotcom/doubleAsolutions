Consulting Services Agreement

This Agreement is entered into between A³ Solutions ("Consultant") and the client ("Client").

1. Services Provided

Consultant agrees to provide the following professional IT consulting services:

  • Microsoft 365 architecture, configuration, and optimization
  • Security assessment and implementation
  • Infrastructure design and strategy
  • PowerShell automation and scripting
  • Technical documentation and knowledge transfer

2. Scope of Work

Specific deliverables, timelines, and project scope will be defined in a separate Statement of Work (SOW) or project proposal. Any changes to scope require written approval from both parties.

3. Fees and Payment Terms

Client agrees to compensate Consultant according to the rates and terms specified in the applicable SOW or proposal. Standard terms include:

  • Hourly consulting rate or fixed project fee as agreed
  • Payment due within 30 days of invoice date
  • Late payments subject to 1.5% monthly interest charge

4. Term and Termination

This agreement begins on the effective date and continues until services are completed or terminated by either party with 14 days' written notice. Client remains responsible for payment of services rendered prior to termination.

5. Confidentiality

Consultant agrees to maintain confidentiality of all client proprietary information and data accessed during the engagement. This obligation survives termination of this agreement.

6. Intellectual Property

All deliverables created specifically for Client become Client's property upon full payment. Consultant retains rights to general methodologies, tools, and pre-existing intellectual property.

7. Limitation of Liability

Consultant's liability for any claims arising from services provided under this agreement shall not exceed the total fees paid by Client for the specific engagement giving rise to the claim.

8. Independent Contractor

Consultant is an independent contractor. Nothing in this agreement creates an employment, partnership, or agency relationship between the parties.
